## Change

Modern tools/list adds output schemas for reinforce_memory and forget_memory.
They describe the existing API1 ReinforceResult and {id,deleted} successes and
the raw native_lifecycle::Reply failure, including its nullable tuple scope and
complete receipt. This is not a contract2 envelope. Legacy-era omission of
outputSchema/structuredContent, existing plain-text errors and all producer,
storage, observation, serializer and transport behavior remain unchanged.

The four known receipt variants are legacyFeedbackRecorded,
lifecycleFeedbackApplied, memoryTombstoned and nativeMemoryTombstoned. The schema
follows their full typed codec shapes. Existing Remote invalid-response handling
can retain a matching typed receipt whose proof is invalid, so canonical state
ranges, single-record assumptions and absence of extra edges cannot be asserted
by this schema. Schema acceptance is not authentication, storage-proof validity,
current-operation binding or readiness. Original receipt attribution is unchanged.

Finite f32 limits follow the actual typed JSON emitter. Its extrema are
+/-3.4028235e38, not the exact f64 widening +/-3.4028234663852886e38. The schema
derives the bound from serde_json's f32 endpoint spelling. No tolerance, numeric
coercion, receipt/hash rounding or global serializer change was introduced.
